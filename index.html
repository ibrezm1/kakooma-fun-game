How to Play Kakooma

1
Inspect Each Outer Wheel:

Every outer group contains 4 or 5 numbers. Exactly ONE number is the sum (or product) of two other numbers in that wheel.

3 + 5 = 8 ➜ 8 is the Kakooma Number!
2
Click the Kakooma Number:

When you click the correct number, watch it fly straight into the Final Center Vault!

3
Solve All 4 Groups:

Once you solve all 4 outer wheels, the 4 winning numbers assemble in the center vault!

👑
Unlock & Conquer the Master Kakooma:

Find the one number in the Center Vault that is the sum of two other center numbers to stop the clock and claim victory!
